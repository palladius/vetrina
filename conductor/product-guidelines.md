# Product Guidelines: Palladius Vetrina

## Voice & Tone
- **Playful yet Polished**: Fun, warm, family-oriented tone with cheeky humor (e.g. Ermete Bottazzi's veganiga, Macha lattes token metrics, Nonna AI commentary).
- **Concise UI**: 1-line taglines, clean emojis, informative hover drawers.

## Design & UI System
- **Palette**: Slate/indigo backdrop with vibrant accents (emerald for games, purple for educational, amber for diaries/tools, pink for silly games).
- **Typography**: Inter / Outfit fonts for modern legibility and Playfair Display / Fun fonts for headlines.
- **Micro-interactions**: Hover drawers expanding smoothly with cubic-bezier transitions without shifting neighbor grid cells (`items-start`).
